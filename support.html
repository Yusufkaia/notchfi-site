Frequently asked questions

Why don't I see 5G/LTE bars when I use iPhone Hotspot?

Apple doesn't let Mac apps read an iPhone's cellular signal. On iPhone Hotspot, NotchFi shows the strength of the Wi-Fi link between your Mac and your iPhone, and labels it that way.

Why is my Wi-Fi network name hidden?

macOS shares the network name only with apps that have Location access. Allow it in NotchFi > Settings > General, or in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. NotchFi never uses your location.

I can't see the island at the top of the screen.

Check that "Show Island at the Top of the Screen" is on in NotchFi > Settings > Island. With several displays, the island appears on the display with a notch; without one, it sits at the top center of your main display.

How does NotchFi count mobile data?

It adds up the bytes your Mac sends and receives while connected to a hotspot, using the network interface counters macOS keeps. Your carrier may count slightly differently, so treat it as a close estimate.

Why did a speed test fail?

The test needs a connection to speed.cloudflare.com. VPNs, firewalls or captive Wi-Fi portals can block it. Hover over the speed test button to see the reason.

How do I reset NotchFi?

Quit NotchFi from its menu, then delete it from Applications. Its settings and usage history are removed with it.
